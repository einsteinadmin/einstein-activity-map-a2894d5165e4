How this map was built

  1. Position. One sentence on who we serve and what we give up.
  2. Themes. The few big promises that position depends on. These are the dark circles.
  3. Trade-offs. For each theme: what do we refuse, and what does that cost us?
  4. Activities. Sort every activity under a theme, including ones other movers also do. The test is whether it serves the position and strengthens a theme.
  5. Lines. Draw one only where removing a piece would weaken another, and write the reason.

One change from the article's format: refusals are shown on a card under each theme, with their cost, instead of as separate circles. Every theme has to show its trade-off.
